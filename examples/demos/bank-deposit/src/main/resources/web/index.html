02

Cash deposit

The scenario checks eligibility, allocates repayments, settles quantum funds and saves the resulting postings.

The default example is a same-branch teller deposit in USD. The advanced panel exposes eligibility, fee, authorization and posting inputs for rejection tests. Alternate currency codes retain the original bank rules; this demo does not convert between currencies.

03

Cash reversal

Apply the original settlement components to the current accounts. A preview moves no funds.

The business date above is the reversal date. Preview does not reserve funds; the submitted reversal checks eligibility again.
